Summary

The App works fully offline. We do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. The App does not contain advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and it does not require an account.

Data stored on your device

The App saves the following only on your own device so it can work: your last selected sounds and volume, your volume setting, and the nap log entries (start and end times) you create. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage deletes it.

Permissions

  • Foreground service (media playback), wake lock: to keep sounds playing while the screen is off.
  • Notifications: to show playback controls while sound is playing.
